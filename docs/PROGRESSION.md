# Progression audit: vanilla necessity -> backrooms source

Every item required to "complete Minecraft" needs a reliable source inside the
backrooms. This is working checklist. Dashes are missing sources.

Beta note: Level 0 is also available as `noclip:backrooms` from normal worlds
through the Noclip Key. Until this checklist closes, that is the recommended
survival route for players who want vanilla completion plus Backrooms
exploration.

## Philosophy: vanilla loops first

The appeal of challenge worlds like skyblock is that the knowledge transfers:
iron comes from zombies or an iron farm, redstone from witches, food from
building a pasture and letting passive mobs spawn. So the order of preference
for every row below is:

1. **Pure vanilla loop.** Mob drops, farming, trading, fishing, curing. The
   generator's only job is to provide the substrate the loop needs (dark cells
   for hostile spawns, water, dirt and grass, a lava pool somewhere).
2. **Generator substrate.** Things worldgen must place because vanilla can't
   create them from nothing: certain stone types, portal blocks, water in
   flooded rooms, lava in maintenance rooms, dirt in overgrown rooms.
3. **Loot.** Chest items that can kickstart loops, but not replace them: Seeds, saddles.
4. **Custom mechanic.** Last resort, only where no vanilla bridge exists.

Modded compatibility is part of this philosophy: automation mods (Create in
particular) interact with the world through vanilla-shaped surfaces, so loops
built on real spawns, real blocks, and real fluids stay automatable for free.

Status: `[ ]` unsourced, `[~]` designed but not implemented, `[x]` implemented.
